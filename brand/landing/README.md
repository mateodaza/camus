# Landing set — six pieces

Cover-grammar studies for the landing. Inspired by the Vintage International
Camus paperbacks — **inspired by, never copied**: all geometry here is original
and stays inside the locked brand grammar (ink `#0B0B16` on paper `#F4F1E8`,
half-squares and circles, 0/45/90 degrees only, no gradients, no outlines).
All are 400×600 (2:3 cover ratio). Generated; safe to scale to any size.

| File | Riff on | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| `the-glare.svg` | *The Stranger* | The indifferent sun in a silent void; the glare is a halftone of half-squares, density stepping with distance. |
| `the-cascade.svg` | *The Myth of Sisyphus* | The great slope and the boulder mid-ascent; half-size echoes of the mountain cascade down the sky. |
| `the-descent.svg` | *The Fall* | Squares and diamonds nested by exact halves, drifting down-right, ending at the boulder. |
| `the-crossing.svg` | *The First Man* | One road per legal direction, meeting at a clearing that holds the boulder. |
| `the-break.svg` | *The Rebel* | A stripe field inverted (XOR) wherever two diagonal bands cross it; where the bands meet each other, order returns. |
| `the-climb.svg` | ours | Still frame of the hero animation: the boulder bedded on the corner-to-corner slope. |

The boulder circle recurs in five of six — it is the thread that makes the set
ours rather than pastiche. `the-break` deliberately has none.

Usage notes
- Mono only as shipped. If one piece carries the screen's single coral accent
  (per BRAND.md: one coral element per screen or none), swap the boulder
  `fill` to `#E8584F` in that one piece only.
- `the-descent` is ink-dominant; balance it with paper-heavy neighbors to keep
  the ~60/35 paper/ink ratio at screen level.
- Pair with `camus_ascending_treadmill.html` (animated hero): `the-climb` is
  its still and works as the reduced-motion / static fallback.
