# Camus — brand

Minimal but forceful. Elegant while contemporary. Like the man.

## Essence

Camus is a tool that refuses to flatter itself: the loop works, the gates doubt it,
the tests decide. The brand carries that same posture — high contrast, no ornament,
nothing that needs to raise its voice. The visual language is borrowed from mid-century
literary modernism (the Vintage International cover grammar: half-square triangles,
ink on paper, a serif that means it) — **inspired by, never copied**.

## The reference

> The struggle itself toward the heights is enough to fill a man's heart.
> One must imagine Sisyphus happy.
> — Albert Camus, *The Myth of Sisyphus* (tr. Justin O'Brien)

Usage: the full two-sentence epigraph appears once per surface, at the end (footer,
last slide, bottom of README). The short form — **"One must imagine Sisyphus happy."** —
may stand alone as the closing line near a CTA. Never both on the same screen; never
as a headline; never paraphrased.

## Marks (decided 2026-06-10)

| | Name | Role |
|---|---|---|
| A | **The Slope** | **Canonical mark.** The mountain and the boulder, mid-ascent — the Sisyphus reference itself. Logo, favicon, wordmark lockup. |
| C | **The Rounds** | **Secondary motif.** Section dividers, loading/progress states, round counters in run UIs. Never competes with A on the same surface at similar scale. |
| B | **The Collar** | Retired (kept in `brand/` as an exploration; may return as an "author portrait" accent if refined). |

Rules for the chosen mark: single ink, no gradients, no outlines, no rotation.
Clear space = the mark's own height / 4 on all sides. Inverts cleanly (paper mark
on ink ground). 45° angles only — every shape is a half-square or a circle.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Ink** | `#0B0B16` | Text, marks, dominant blocks. This is a very dark warm navy, not pure black. |
| **Paper** | `#F4F1E8` | Ground. Warm off-white, never pure white. |
| **Coral** | `#E8584F` | Accent (decided 2026-06-10) — links, the CTA, the active state. From the portrait ground. Use in ONE place per screen, or not at all. Mono-only remains the rule for README/terminal surfaces. |

Ratio discipline: ~60% paper, ~35% ink, ≤5% coral. A screen with no coral at all is
always acceptable; a screen with two coral elements is not.

## Typography

- **Display serif** — headlines, the epigraph: `Source Serif 4` (Google Fonts), or
  system Georgia as fallback. Large, tight-leaded, sentence case. Italic only for
  the quote.
- **Grotesque sans** — UI, body, labels: `Archivo` (or `Inter`). Generous line-height.
- **Mono** — code, terminal blocks, metadata chips: system mono stack.
- Caps + letterspacing (`0.3em`) reserved for the wordmark CAMUS and tiny labels only.

## Voice

Declarative sentences. Short ones. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "blazingly."
Claims are verifiable or absent: say "159 assertions, 9 suites," not "battle-tested."
The product admits its limits in the same breath as its strengths ("trusted-code tool
only") — honesty IS the differentiator, in copy as in architecture. Philosophy is
seasoning, not the meal: one Camus reference per surface, carried by the work, never
winking.

## Provenance note

The triangle grammar is inspired by the Vintage International paperback covers
(an established design language, not ours). All marks here are original geometry;
the silhouette option is an original abstraction, not a traced photograph.
